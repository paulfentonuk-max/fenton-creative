Our Mission

FENTON CREATIVE™ was founded with a clear mission: to make AI interactions secure, private, and cost-effective. We believe that organizations should be able to leverage the power of artificial intelligence without compromising sensitive data or breaking the bank.

Our local-first, edge-native architecture ensures that your data never leaves your control. With AEGIS™, we've created the first complete platform for AI protection that combines data loss prevention, cost optimization, and encrypted communications.
