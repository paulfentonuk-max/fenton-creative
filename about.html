About Fenton Creative™

Pioneering the future of AI-powered web security

Our Story

Fenton Creative™ was founded with a clear mission: to make the web safer and faster through intelligent AI-driven solutions. What began as a research project into WebAssembly-based security has evolved into a comprehensive suite of products protecting applications worldwide.

Our team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and high-performance computing to deliver solutions that don't just meet industry standards—they redefine them.
